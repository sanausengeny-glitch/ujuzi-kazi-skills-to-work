Ujuzi Kazi compares the skills in local job adverts with the skills young people actually have. Young people see which training opens the most jobs. Training partners see where to put their money.

The problem we’re solving

Training follows habit, not jobs

Many young people train in the same few skills, while local employers struggle to fill roles that need different ones.

Nobody sees the whole picture

Job adverts and youth data sit in different places. Without putting them side by side, the mismatch stays invisible.

Rural youth get the least guidance

Career advice and labour-market data focus on cities. A young person in a rural county has little to go on when choosing training.

SDG 8: Decent work and economic growth

Ujuzi Kazi targets one SDG and two of its targets.

Target 8.5

Achieve full and productive employment and decent work for all women and men, including for young people.

How we contribute: young people choose training that matches real local jobs.

Target 8.6

Substantially reduce the proportion of youth not in employment, education or training.

How we contribute: training partners aim courses at skills that lead to work, so fewer young people train for jobs that aren’t there.

Data charter

Data about young people looking for work is personal. These rules follow the principles of Kenya’s Data Protection Act, 2019.

Skills checks stay private

The skills a young person picks are used in their own browser to show results. They aren’t sent or stored anywhere else.

Profiles are anonymous

Youth profiles hold county, age, sex, education, status and skills. No names, phone numbers or ID numbers.

Consent for any survey

Real youth profiles are only collected from people who agree, and only shown as counts and shares.

Small groups stay hidden

In a real deployment, any breakdown with fewer than 5 people is hidden so no one can be identified.

Public adverts only

Job adverts come from public listings. We record the skills asked for, not the employer’s private details.

Honest about the data

The dataset in this prototype is generated. Every page says so until real data is loaded.
